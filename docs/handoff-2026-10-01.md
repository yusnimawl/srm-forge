## What v0a-2 does (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-v0a2-turns-after-map-design.md`)
- One FEM map per geometry at `REFERENCE_TURNS = 20` (generator, `full` preset, 30 × 20 grid). `turns.rescale_turns` gives the exact map at any turn count (currents/k, ψ·k, L_inc·k²); a slow FEM test confirms it to 1e-6.
- **Wire mode** (default when `Requirements.wires` + `phase_current_a` are given): wires by dimensions (`srmapp/wire.py`: round, rectangular, litz). For each wire and turn count, as many parallel strands as fit in `max_slot_fill` (0.6), current density ≤ `j_limit`. Picks the feasible winding with the **lowest resistance**.
- **Density mode**: fixed J, picks the feasible winding with the **most turns** within the optional `phase_current_a` cap; scan up to 500 turns (note when the choice hits it).
- Map current range in wire mode = exact max N·I over the allowed windings; stage 1 drops designs with no possible winding and checks iron mass only; stage 2 checks total mass after the winding is chosen.
- Results: `Evaluation.winding` (turns, strands, R, J, current, copper mass), `scan` curve, status `no_winding` as a fallback.
