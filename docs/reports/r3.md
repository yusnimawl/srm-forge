# R3: Losses and drive tail

Merged 2026-10-20, PR #4. Versions after the merge: srmcore, srmapp, srmweb and `web/` 0.6.0.
Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-r3-losses-design.md`. Plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-r3-losses.md`. Detailed results `docs/r3-loss-results.md`, sources `docs/r3-sources.md`, release notes `docs/release-notes-0.6.0.md`.

## Goal

Compute copper and iron loss and an efficiency map, and let the drive run past the aligned position. Done when efficiency at any operating point is compared with a published case and the error is reported.

## What shipped

- `materials/loss.py`: Bertotti fit to a loss table, valid to 1.3 T for 10JNEX900. `post/iron_loss.py`: per-element loss of a B(t) waveform.
- `maps/stroke.py`: field solves along the stroke with all phases on, cached.
- `srmapp/copper.py` (skin and proximity loss, wire mode) and `srmapp/losses.py` (`efficiency_map`, 6 speeds by 5 torque levels).
- Drive tail past aligned, counted with its negative torque. This changes search rankings.
- Winding temperature as a requirements input, default 120 degC (was a fixed 100 degC).
- Web: job kind `losses`, routes `/api/cases/{id}/losses`, and the Losses tab.
- `examples/materu_losses_check.py` for the check against Materu's measured 6/4.

## Decisions

- 2026-10-18, owner: iron loss runs in a separate `losses` job (evaluate keeps its cost).
- 2026-10-18, owner: the 10JNEX900 fit is widened to 1.3 T, the high-B flag is a loss share, and over-prediction below 0.2 T is accepted.
- 2026-10-18, owner: friction and windage are excluded (added later, tracker 10m).
- 2026-10-19, owner: the Materu check runs at `full`, not `reference` (1.2 M-node mesh, about 70 h and out of memory at 10 workers).
