Merged 2026-11-05 to 2026-11-06, PRs #18, #19 and #20. Versions after the merge: srmcore 0.7.0 (unchanged).
Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-r4-end-effects-design.md`. Plans `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-r4a-3d-reference-model.md` and `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-r4b-fail-measured-gap.md`. Detailed results `docs/r4-end-effect-results.md`.

## Goal

Build a 3D NGSolve reference model, fit an end-effect correction to it, and bring the measured comparisons inside the stated uncertainty. Done when the measured comparisons fall inside the stated uncertainty, or the remaining gap is stated in the budget.

## What shipped

- `tools/end3d`: a 3D reference model (swept prism mesh), gates G0 to G2, and a `max_dofs` size guard (later, PR #28).
- Gate G1 failed. No correction layer was built (spec decision 3).
- `MEASURED_GAP` in `maps/validation.py`: the measured TEAM 24 and Krishnan 6/4 residuals now enter the `end_effect` budget row, added as g / (1 - g).
- Budget row `measured_lu` (`MEASURED_LU_GAP`, the Krishnan Lu residual, on Lu only).
- 8/6 validation re-run, results in `docs/r4-end-effect-results.md`.

## Decisions

- 2026-11-03, owner: the correction's constants come from 3D runs only. TEAM 24 and Krishnan are a check, never a calibration.
- 2026-11-05, builder: G0 is read at the coarsest level that agrees with the finest within 0.1 % (ruling R20).
- 2026-11-05, builder: G1 failed, so R4 stops and the measured gap goes into the budget.
- 2026-11-06, owner: the Krishnan Lu residual gets its own budget row, `measured_lu`, 31.13 % of the model value.
- 2026-11-06, owner: R5's EM torque row leaves out the measured gap and uses a floor of 10 % (factor 1.10 on the 8/6). The model is low on torque in every steady-state comparison.
