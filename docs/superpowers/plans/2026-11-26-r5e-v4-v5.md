# R5e gates V4 (Kiyota 2014 peak) and V5 (thermal mesh) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Run gate V4: the rating's thermal network on the Kiyota 18/12 with natural cooling, the Fig. 8 current
trace and the measured resistance, against the measured coil-end rise. Run gate V5: the hot-spot change of the
rating's thermal network from `screening` to `full` to `reference` on the 8/6 and the Kiyota. Record both verdicts
and put the results into the `end_winding_model` and `thermal_mesh` budget rows (spec §7).

**Architecture:** a test-side helper `tests/kiyota_thermal.py` builds the Kiyota network with the rating's own code
(`thermal_domain`, `coefficients`, `build_network`, `PointHeat`, `load_fn`) and steps the Fig. 8 current trace
through one `ThermalSolver.transient` call. `examples/kiyota_thermal_check.py` runs V4 and writes
`docs/validation/r5_v4_kiyota.json`. `examples/thermal_mesh_check.py` runs V5 on fixed loads and writes
`docs/validation/r5_v5_mesh.json`. No EM solve runs in either gate. srmcore does not change. srmapp changes only in
one note (Task 7), or in Task 6 if its condition holds.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12+, numpy, scipy.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-r5-thermal-stress-design.md` §7 (rows `end_winding_model`,
`thermal_mesh`), §8 gates V4 and V5, §14 (V4 is a weak check of cooling). Inputs: `docs/r5-sources.md` §9.2 and
§9.3. Scope: roadmap §0, Bike motor v1 part 1 (V2 gap accepted, V6 on the Kiyota rotor replaced by part 5).

## Findings from the diagnosis (2026-11-26, inline prototype)

Prototype scripts in `/tmp/v4v5/` (not committed): `proto*.py` (V4), `v5proto.py` (V5), `meshsize.py`. They use the
loads of this plan except where a row says otherwise. Iron "0.7/0.3" means 70 % in the stator iron, 30 % in the
rotor iron, uniform per unit volume.

1. **V4 is a check of adiabatic copper heating.** The Fig. 8 hold is 140.7 A RMS in 9 parallel 0.9 mm strands, so
   J = 24.6 A/mm². The integral of I² dt over the V0 trace is 11.12 s x 140.7². DC copper heating with no heat
   flow, from 51.1 degC, with the library copper (8890 kg/m3, 385.2 J/(kg K), 1.7241e-8 Ohm m at 20 degC,
   0.00393 1/K), gives 40.56 K at 14 s. The measured rise is 39.5 +- 0.4 K (trace) and
   41 K (printed). This needs no model input except the cited wire data.
2. **Our network with the measured resistance gives 42.66 K** (end-lump peak, copper only, `screening` and `full`
   the same to 0.01 K, steps of 0.05 s and 0.025 s the same to 0.01 K). The 5.2 % above item 1 follows from the ratio (5.6 %) of
   the measured 97.8 mOhm to the resistance of our mean turn length (92.6 mOhm at 20 degC, mean turn 256.3 mm). The
   measured value implies 271.3 mm. Kiyota's own prediction is 91.7 mOhm. The measured value most likely includes
   the leads (an inference).
3. **The sensor lags.** Measured rise 22.7 K at 10 s and 35.5 K at 14 s, ours 32.8 K and 42.7 K. The measured trace
   keeps rising for 4 s after the current stops. The paper does not give the sensor type or position. So only the
   peak rise can be compared. Time-matched values are reported, not judged.
4. **Zero iron capacity sets the result once iron loss is present.** 10JNEX900 has no cited specific heat, so the
   rating gives the iron zero capacity (`thermal_network.py`). The iron loss then goes into the copper at once.

   | Case (`screening`, measured R, nominal rows unless noted) | End-lump peak rise (K) | Hot-spot peak rise (K) | Hot spot at 20 s |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | copper only | 42.66 | 42.66 | end winding |
   | iron 0.6 kW, 0.7/0.3 | 44.37 | 49.60 | coil side |
   | iron 2.0 kW, 0.7/0.3 | 49.39 | 73.66 | coil side |
   | iron 4.4 kW, 0.7/0.3 | 57.99 | 114.90 | coil side |
   | iron 4.4 kW, 0.7/0.3, lamination c 438 J/(kg K) x 7650 kg/m3 | 41.73 | 41.73 | end winding |
   | copper only, every row unfavourable | 42.67 | 42.70 | coil side |
   | this plan's nominal (iron 4.4 kW / 3.2 in the stator, 573 W windage in the rotor) | 49.24 | 73.01 | coil side |
   | this plan's rated set (every row unfavourable, iron x 3.2, windage x 1.2) | 63.30 | 183.86 | coil side |
   | this plan's nominal, lamination c 438 J/(kg K) x 7490 kg/m3 | 41.60 | 41.60 | end winding |

   With a lamination capacity the iron loss moves the coil-end peak by about 1 K. Without it the iron loss sets the
   result, and the slot hot spot rises 73 K (nominal) to 184 K (rated) in 20 s.
5. **The conduction rows do not reach the 14 s peak.** Copper only, every row unfavourable: 42.67 K against 42.66 K.
   V4 cannot check cross-wire, liner, contact, air gap, cooling h or lamination k (spec §14 confirmed).
6. **No stroke at the Fig. 8 point.** The drive model reaches about 50 kW single pulse on this machine
   (docs/r3-loss-results.md, Kiyota finding 2). 109 kW needs continuous conduction, which is Bike motor v1 part 2.
   The cached Kiyota maps in the main checkout are srmcore 0.7.0, and the provenance includes the version, so an
   R3 iron loss would also need a new `full` map (hours). Items 4 and 6 together: V4 takes the iron magnitude from
   the measurement (Call 1).
7. **V5, 8/6 fixture** (rating network, placeholder jacket, 2000 r/min coefficients, 24 turns of 1 mm wire,
   10 A RMS steady, 20 A RMS for the 30 s peak, iron 30 W 0.7/0.3), hot spot in degC:

   | Preset | Nodes | Nominal steady | Nominal peak | Rated steady | Rated peak |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | screening | 31 543 | 96.894 | 120.733 | 134.801 | 161.170 |
   | full | 57 987 | 96.934 | 120.779 | 134.881 | 161.270 |
   | reference | 405 997 | 96.999 | 120.853 | 135.016 | 161.429 |
   | screening - reference | | -0.105 | -0.120 | -0.215 | -0.259 |
   | full - reference | | -0.065 | -0.074 | -0.135 | -0.159 |

   The largest change is 0.26 K at `screening`. The rating's bisection stops within `TOL_K` = 0.5 K below the
   limit, so a change of this size does not move a rating. The `screening` preset is not the `full` knobs at a
   larger scale, so the three levels do not follow one h² line.
8. **Kiyota steady with the placeholder jacket:** 60 A RMS with 1.5 kW iron (0.7/0.3) runs away (over 1000 degC).
   30 A with 300 W stator iron gives 99.7 degC. Task 4 sets the V5 Kiyota load by one probe (rule in Task 4).
   A runaway at this load on a jacket-cooled 264 mm machine is a rating-model observation, out of scope here. At
   60 A without iron loss the end-space air reaches 132 degC and the end winding 194 degC. That path has no cited
   scatter (docs/r5-thermal-results.md, "Unresolved sources").
9. **Sizes and times** (LattePanda, one thread for the mesh): Kiyota thermal domain 73 879 nodes (`screening`,
   28 s), 135 396 (`full`, 55 s), 951 237 (`reference`, 742 s). 8/6: 31 543 (9 s), 57 987 (17 s), 405 997 (252 s).
   One 20 s V4 transient (400 steps of 0.05 s, the load rebuilt each step) takes 27 s on `screening` and 80 to
   95 s on `full`. The 8/6 V5 set takes 16 s (`screening`), 38 s (`full`) and 313 s (`reference`, mesh included).

## Calls made in this plan (not in the spec)

1. **Iron at the Fig. 8 point from the measurement.** Spec V4 says "losses from our R3 model at the Fig. 8 point".
   The drive model has no stroke there (item 6). `K_IRON_HI` is defined on this machine as measured "iron and other"
   over the model (docs/r3-loss-results.md), so the rating's nominal iron at 1.5 kHz is about M / 3.2 and its rated
   iron is M, where M is the measured "iron and other". V4 uses that: nominal iron = M / `k_iron_hi`(1500 Hz), and
   `coefficients` applies the factor in the rated set. M = 4.4 kW, Table III (b) (130 A RMS, the measured point
   nearest the test current, 8700 r/min, which the text calls 7500 r/min). Input row: M = 0.6 kW, Table III (c)
   (7500 r/min, light load). The iron goes into the stator iron, uniform per unit volume (the rotor is behind the
   air gap, so this is the hot side for the coil. This is an assumption).
2. **Compared quantity: the end-lump peak rise.** The sensor is on the coil end (2014 p. 5716), so ours is the
   hottest end-winding lump. Ours = its maximum over 0 to 20 s minus the start temperature. The rating's hot spot
   (coil sides included) is reported beside it and not judged (nothing was measured in the slot).
3. **Target: 39.5 +- 0.4 K**, the trace reading (docs/r5-sources.md §9.2: the figure's own arrow spans 39.7 K). The
   printed 41 K gets its own verdict line beside it.
4. **Start state:** uniform 51.1 degC (the Fig. 8 start reading) at every node and free lump. Input row: copper
   nodes and end lumps at 51.1 degC, everything else at the ambient.
5. **Ambient 25 degC** (not printed, an assumption). Input row: 40 degC.
6. **Mechanical loss:** the measured 573 W at 7493 r/min (Fig. 7) as windage in the rotor iron (`PointHeat.windage_w`).
   The rated set takes x 1.2 (the paper's 20 % bearing-condition scatter). Input row: the same 573 W as bearing
   friction at the end caps (`PointHeat.friction_w`).
7. **The `em_torque` row has no effect in V4:** the current is imposed, not found from a torque. State it in the JSON.
8. **Band and pass rule** (spec: "measured 41 K inside our predicted band", and "the band includes input rows ... each from a
   perturbation run"): low = nominal + the sum of the negative row changes - numerical error, high = rated + the sum of
   the positive row changes + numerical error. Numerical error = |full - reference| + |dt - dt/2| on `full`. Pass
   when low - 0.4 <= 39.5 <= high + 0.4. Rows are the worst-case stack, as in spec §7.
9. **Residual into the `end_winding_model` row:** r = (39.5 - nominal) / nominal on the end-lump peak rise. If
   r <= 0 the model reads hot (the safe side), the row stays 0 and its note states the hot-side residual. If r > 0,
   Task 6 runs.
10. **V5 loads are fixed and the same on every mesh:** DC copper at a set RMS current through `load_fn`, iron of a
    set total, uniform per part. This isolates the thermal discretisation. The mesh error of the losses themselves
    belongs to the EM and iron rows.
11. **V5 into the `thermal_mesh` row:** e_p = the largest |p - reference| over both machines, both sets (nominal,
    rated) and both quantities (steady, peak), for p = `screening` and `full`. If every e_p <= `TOL_K` (0.5 K, the
    rating's own stop tolerance), the row stays 0 and its note states e_p. Otherwise Task 6 runs. The reference has an
    error of its own (P1, about 1/8 of the `full` error if the error scales with h², an inference), so e_p reads
    slightly low. The note says so.
12. **Iron capacity is an input row, not a fix.** V4 runs the rating as built (zero iron capacity) as its nominal.
    Row: c = 438 J/(kg K) (Faiz 2009 Table II, a lamination of unstated grade, cited in docs/r5-sources.md §9.1) times
    the 10JNEX900 density 7490 kg/m3. Whether the library takes a specific heat for 10JNEX900 is an owner decision
    (tracker 10d), not part of this plan.

## Risks

- **V4 passes on a wide band.** The rated set reaches 63 K on the end lump, so the band will hold the measurement
  almost whatever happens. The residual r of the nominal is the number that matters. State both.
- **The nominal depends on M.** With zero iron capacity, M of 0.6 to 4.4 kW moves the end-lump peak by several K
  and the slot hot spot by tens of K. The JSON and the results section must show this, not only the verdict.
- **Reference memory.** The 951 237-node transient was not run in the diagnosis. If it needs more than 24 GB, run
  `reference` for the nominal set only and state the gap.
- **Task 1 changes an input.** If the review moves the Kiyota geometry or the 3.2 factor, the diagnosis numbers
  move too. The smoke checks in Tasks 3 and 4 then compare with the new values, recorded in the commit message.
